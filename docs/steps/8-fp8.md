# Step 8: FP8 (E4M3) tensor cores

Source: [src/fp8.cu](../../src/fp8.cu). RTX 4060, 4096³: 57,778 GFLOPS, 93.3% of cuBLASLt FP8 on the same E4M3 codes, and 2.0× step 7's FP16 kernel.

## What changed

Step 7's pipeline stays. WMMA has no FP8 fragments, so the math is `mma.sync.m16n8k32` E4M3 PTX (`QMMA.16832.F32.E4M3.E4M3` in SASS). `ldmatrix.x4` delivers 8-bit fragments directly but cannot transpose them, so B is stored K-major (N x K), the TN layout cuBLASLt FP8 also requires. Inputs are quantized per tensor (`x * 448 / amax`); the quantizers match chip-lab's C++ models byte for byte ([cross-check](../results/quant-crosscheck.csv)).

## The tensor core truncates its sum

The first version's error grew with K. The [DeepSeek-V3 technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19437) found the same on Hopper: FP8 accumulation keeps about 14 bits, so it promotes to FP32 every 128 elements of K. `check/mma_probe.cu` measures it independently on Ada with hand-built operands ([output](../results/mma-probe-rtx4060.csv)):

- The sum keeps 13 bits below the largest addend's leading bit (14 with it): 2¹³ + 1 = 8193, but 2¹⁴ + 1 = 16384.
- Dropped bits are truncated toward −∞: −4096 + 0.75 gives −4095.5.
- Each addend is truncated on its own: 2¹⁴ plus 32 products of 1 gives 2¹⁴, not 2¹⁴ + 32.

New here are the Ada measurement and the cost of each promotion interval. Each 64-deep K tile is summed in a fresh accumulator, then added in FP32 registers ([ablation](../results/fp8-promotion.csv)):

| Promotion | TFLOP/s | Max error, n = 4096 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Never | 53.9 | 2.4e-3, grows with K |
| Every 64-deep K tile | 55.7 | 6–9e-5, flat in K |
| After every MMA | 47.6 | 4–6e-5 |
| cuBLASLt FP8 | 60.6 | 1.0–1.4e-4 |

Error is against exact FP64 dot products of the same codes. Every tile costs nothing measurable. Promoting after every MMA doubles the FADDs and costs 15%.

## Accuracy on the chip-lab distributions

`bench numerics` reruns chip-lab's GEMM-error experiment on its 2,000 vector pairs ([results](../results/numerics-rtx4060.csv)). Median / p99 of |y − y₆₄| / Σ|aᵢbᵢ| at K = 4096:

| Format | Gaussian | Outliers | chip-lab model, Gaussian |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| BF16 (cuBLAS) | 3.6e-5 / 1.4e-4 | 1.9e-4 / 7.9e-4 | 3.6e-5 / 1.4e-4 |
| E4M3 per tensor (this kernel) | 6.2e-4 / 2.3e-3 | 3.0e-3 / 1.1e-2 | 6.2e-4 / 2.3e-3 |

The GPU matches the bit-exact C++ model to two digits: at this K, input rounding dominates the error.

## Cost of quantizing

Quantizing both 4096² operands takes 1.25 ms against 2.41 ms of GEMM (52%). The pass is memory-bound: it reads the FP32 inputs twice (amax, then convert). Training would fuse it into the producing kernel.

## What limits it now

The tensor pipe is 45% busy and math-pipe throttle is the top stall (11.3), the same pattern as step 7. cuBLASLt also stops at 61–63 TFLOP/s with FP32 accumulation, a quarter of the counters' 246 TFLOP/s FP8 peak. Profile at 4096³: 970 FLOP per DRAM byte ([roofline](../roofline.png), [counters](../results/ncu-rtx4060.csv)). Process notes: [journal](../journal/2026-11-12-fp8.md).
