# Step 8: FP8 (E4M3) tensor cores

Source: [src/fp8.cu](../../src/fp8.cu). RTX 4060, 4096³: 57,778 GFLOPS, 93.3% of cuBLASLt FP8 on the same E4M3 codes, and 2.0× step 7's FP16 kernel.

## What changed

Step 7's pipeline stays. WMMA has no FP8 fragments, so the math is `mma.sync.m16n8k32` E4M3 PTX (`QMMA.16832.F32.E4M3.E4M3` in SASS). `ldmatrix.x4` delivers 8-bit fragments directly but cannot transpose them, so B is stored K-major (N x K), the TN layout cuBLASLt FP8 also requires. Inputs are quantized per tensor (`x * 448 / amax`) by `src/quant.cu`. The quantizers match the chip-lab C++ models byte for byte on 27 million elements ([cross-check](../results/quant-crosscheck.csv)).

## The tensor core truncates its sum

The first version's error grew with K. `check/mma_probe.cu` feeds one MMA hand-built operands ([output](../results/mma-probe-rtx4060.csv)):

- The sum keeps 13 bits below the largest addend's leading bit: 2¹³ + 1 = 8193, but 2¹⁴ + 1 = 16384.
- Dropped bits are truncated toward −∞: −4096 + 0.75 gives −4095.5.
- Each addend is truncated on its own: 2¹⁴ plus 32 products of 1 gives 2¹⁴, not 2¹⁴ + 32.

A long accumulator chain therefore drops small terms once the sum grows. The fix is **promotion**: each 64-deep K tile is summed in a fresh accumulator and then added in FP32 registers ([ablation](../results/fp8-promotion.csv)):

| Promotion | TFLOP/s | Max error, n = 4096 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Never | 53.9 | 2.4e-3, grows with K |
| Every 64-deep K tile | 55.7 | 6–9e-5, flat in K |
| After every MMA | 47.6 | 4–6e-5 |
| cuBLASLt FP8 | 60.6 | 1.0–1.4e-4 |

Error is against exact FP64 dot products of the same codes. Promoting every tile costs nothing measurable. Promoting after every MMA doubles the FADDs and costs 15%.

## Accuracy on the chip-lab distributions

`bench numerics` reruns chip-lab's GEMM-error experiment on the same 2,000 vector pairs ([results](../results/numerics-rtx4060.csv)). Median / p99 of |y − y₆₄| / Σ|aᵢbᵢ| at K = 4096:

| Format | Gaussian | Outliers | chip-lab model, Gaussian |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| BF16 (cuBLAS) | 3.6e-5 / 1.4e-4 | 1.9e-4 / 7.9e-4 | 3.6e-5 / 1.4e-4 |
| E4M3 per tensor (this kernel) | 6.2e-4 / 2.3e-3 | 3.0e-3 / 1.1e-2 | 6.2e-4 / 2.3e-3 |

The GPU matches the bit-exact C++ model to two digits: at this K, input rounding dominates the error.

## Cost of quantizing

At 4096, quantizing both operands takes 1.25 ms against 2.41 ms for the GEMM, or 52%. The pass is memory-bound: it reads the FP32 inputs twice (amax, then convert). Training would fuse it into the producing kernel.

## What limits it now

The tensor pipe is 45% busy and math-pipe throttle is the top stall (11.3), the same pattern as step 7. 128 registers and 40,960 bytes of shared memory still allow two blocks per SM. cuBLASLt also stops at 61–63 TFLOP/s with FP32 accumulation, a quarter of the counters' 246 TFLOP/s FP8 peak. Profile at 4096³: 970 FLOP per DRAM byte ([roofline](../roofline.png), [counters](../results/ncu-rtx4060.csv)). Process notes: [journal](../journal/2026-11-12-fp8.md).
